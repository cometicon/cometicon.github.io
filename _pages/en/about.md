I am a Machine Learning Engineer and PhD in Computer Science working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, 3D computer vision, medical imaging and scientific computing.

My work focuses on developing computational methods to analyze, reconstruct and model complex 3D data, combining machine learning, geometric modeling and simulation, and on turning them into robust, deployable software.

During my PhD at Université Grenoble Alpes, in collaboration with Inria (Morpheo team), Anatoscope and Grenoble Alps Hospitals, I worked on the analysis of adolescent scoliosis using anatomical models and motion capture data. This research involved 3D reconstruction, multimodal data analysis, biomechanical modeling and machine learning: deep learning to infer the 3D spine from depth maps of the back, and subject-specific kinematic models for the 4D analysis of the trunk. More details on the [PhD page](https://cometicon.github.io/en/phd).

Most recently, at [Circle](https://www.circle.dental/), I worked on AI and 3D technologies for digital dentistry. I designed and deployed deep learning models for the automated analysis of 3D intraoral scans, built the MLOps pipelines that retrained and shipped them, and facilitated the team’s Agile workflows as Product Owner. These methods bridged research and real-world applications, such as the [Circle-One](https://www.circle.one/) application.

Before my PhD, I developed fast open-source scientific software for molecular phylogeny (Treerecs, Seaview 5). My publications have gathered over 400 citations on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OqDSwDEAAAAJ).

I enjoy working on challenging problems where scientific research, algorithms and software engineering come together, from understanding a problem and designing a computational approach to building a robust solution. Feel free to browse my [CV](https://cometicon.github.io/en/cv) or [get in touch](https://cometicon.github.io/contact).
